# Example: Computer Vision

## Weak starting idea

“Implementing YOLO for rider helmet detection.”

## Diagnosis

Potentially implementation-oriented. “YOLO in a domain” does not establish a computer-software advance or scientific/technological uncertainty.

## Researchable direction

Investigate whether a defined uncertainty-aware temporal fusion mechanism improves helmet detection reliability under low light, motion blur, and partial occlusion versus single-frame YOLO and established video-detection baselines.

## Contribution test

- New mechanism: precisely specify fusion, uncertainty signal, and decision rule.
- Uncertainty: it is unknown whether the mechanism improves reliability across conditions without excessive latency.
- Evidence: condition-stratified mAP/recall/calibration, latency, ablation, repeated runs, and failure analysis.
- Transferability: release protocol/configuration and define scope limits.

## Frascati caution

The artifact can support R&D only if it resolves the qualifying uncertainty and satisfies all five criteria.
